About

I'm Tanvir, a student from Saidpur, Bangladesh. I'm drawn to anything that makes me ask how it works, from the code behind a website to the physics behind a rocket.

I'm drawn to space, aerospace and science, and I look up at the night sky more than I probably should. I also enjoy photography, mostly of the sky, nature and quiet places. Technology, web development and computer science are part of what I like to explore too.

I'm still early in the journey. This site is where I keep what I'm learning.
